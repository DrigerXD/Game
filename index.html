How to play

Crack the case by naming the hidden five-letter word in six guesses. After each guess, the tiles report how close you are.

SGreen — right letter, right spot.
TGold — in the word, different spot.
OGray — not in the word.
  • Unlimited opens a new case every round, drawn from an online dictionary when you’re connected.
  • Daily case is one shared word per day, the same for every detective.
  • Multiplayer creates an invite room for up to four detectives. Everyone shares one board and six guesses.
  • Get a clue reveals one letter, once per case.
  • Hard mode (in settings) forces you to use every clue you’ve found.

Press Enter to submit and Backspace to edit.

Play together

Choose how many players can join your private room. Everyone shares one board and six guesses; each guess appears for the whole team. Keep the host page open until the case ends.
